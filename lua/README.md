# lua

Lua 5.4.8 converted to daslang by [c2das-re](https://github.com/lookibed/c2das-re). Everything in this directory is generated by `scripts/publish_ports.sh --program lua` in c2das-re; never edit it, a fix belongs in c2das-re.

## Variants

- `readable/` - for reading and changing the code: Boris, and the weak-model add-a-feature check.
- `fast/` - for running.

`fast/` is converted with `checks: off`: no null or bounds checks, so C bugs are not caught (a null access is a SIGSEGV; an out-of-bounds index reads neighbour memory; AOT keeps its range checks); use `readable/` to debug.

Each variant converts the same program under its own knobs (c2das-re's `ports/variants/`, copied as its `variant.json` with the reason and the numbers behind each knob). x native is the das time over the native build's, above 1 when slower; the lines are the published tree's.

## Files

- `readable/lua/` - the interpreter `lua`: the core, the standard libraries and `lua.c`, the readable variant, a module per C file, every cycle of files one module of `.das.inc` fragments (c2das-re's source layout; its `program.layout.txt` lists the modules); `readable/lua/lua.das` holds `main`.
- `readable/luac/` - the compiler `luac`: the core, the auxiliary library and `luac.c`, the readable variant, a module per C file, every cycle of files one module of `.das.inc` fragments (c2das-re's source layout; its `program.layout.txt` lists the modules); `readable/luac/luac.das` holds `main`.
- `readable/lua.c2das` - the sidecar the readable variant was converted under, `readable/variant.json` - its knobs with the reason and the numbers behind each.
- `fast/lua/` - the interpreter `lua`: the core, the standard libraries and `lua.c`, the fast variant, a module per C file, every cycle of files one module of `.das.inc` fragments (c2das-re's source layout; its `program.layout.txt` lists the modules); `fast/lua/lua.das` holds `main`.
- `fast/luac/` - the compiler `luac`: the core, the auxiliary library and `luac.c`, the fast variant, a module per C file, every cycle of files one module of `.das.inc` fragments (c2das-re's source layout; its `program.layout.txt` lists the modules); `fast/luac/luac.das` holds `main`.
- `fast/lua.c2das` - the sidecar the fast variant was converted under, `fast/variant.json` - its knobs with the reason and the numbers behind each.
- `LICENSE` - Lua's license, from `src/lua.h` of the release; the conversion is a derived work.
- `bench/` - the benchmark scripts the tables below run, and `timed.lua`, which runs each and reports its work seconds measured inside the process.
- `perf/capture_*.json` - every capture the tables were rendered from, every sample of every lane.

## Running

The modules require c2das-re's `crt` and `c_runtime` modules: a c2das-re checkout at the commit above, with the daScript its `VERSIONS.md` pins built and `scripts/build_crt.sh` run. With `<c2das-re>` that checkout:

```
daslang -load_module <c2das-re>/dasCRuntime -load_module <c2das-re>/c_runtime readable/lua/lua.das -- -e "print(1+1)"
daslang -load_module <c2das-re>/dasCRuntime -load_module <c2das-re>/c_runtime -jit fast/lua/lua.das -- bench/fib.lua
daslang -load_module <c2das-re>/dasCRuntime -load_module <c2das-re>/c_runtime -exe fast/lua/lua.das -output lua && ./lua bench/fib.lua
daslang -load_module <c2das-re>/dasCRuntime -load_module <c2das-re>/c_runtime readable/luac/luac.das -- -o out.luac script.lua
```

Everything after `--` is the program's own command line; daslang writes the `-exe` binary at the `-output` path as given.

Reproduced by `scripts/publish_ports.sh --program lua --no-convert --perf --samples 3 --lanes interp,jit,exe,aot,interp-fast,jit-fast,exe-fast,aot-fast --timeout 1800 --box PC`. A sample is one run of the whole program, its own process spawned by the publisher (the spawn included); a cell is the median of 3 samples, ± half their range as a share of the median; a run past 1800 s fails its cell. A das lane's cell stands only when every run prints what the native build prints and exits as it does (the test-suite row compares the exit code and requires the line the suite prints after its last test passes, not the output: it prints its random seeds and timings). A lane the run left out is `not measured`. A lane `, fast` runs the `fast/` variant's modules, the others the `readable/` variant's. An AOT lane has a column only where its build stood.

Every speed number is the work alone, timed inside the process: a workload runs under `bench/timed.lua`, which reads `os.clock()` (C `clock()`, the process's CPU time, under every lane) before and after the script and reports the seconds between, and its work time is the median of those, ± half their range as a share of it. The work seconds are process CPU time (`clock()`, user plus system), not dasProfile's steady wall clock, since stock Lua has no in-process wall clock finer than `os.time()`'s whole seconds. A cell without them (a capture made before the wrapper) is `(by subtraction)`, its work time the cell less the same lane's `startup` cell, the run that loads the program and does no work, ± the two cells' half ranges as a share of the work time, and the mark follows it into every ratio and mean; one not above 0 is `within the start`, and a lane whose startup cell does not stand has none. The startup cells stand alone in the startup table, their ratio to native's with them, in no other ratio or mean; the times table holds the raw medians, the start included. A cell or a work time whose spread is above 20% is `suspect`: its spread alone, no ratio, and out of the projects table's means; a suspect startup cell makes every work time of its lane by subtraction `suspect (startup)`.
